Developed MAGICAL, a multi-class machine learning classifier to predict synthetic lethal and viable genetic interactions using protein-protein interaction network properties as features. Also carried out survival analysis to depict patient response to genetic interactions.

**Published in:** PLOS Computational Biology, 2024
